An organisation is active as soon as it signs up. Suspending it refuses all of its keys within about 1 minute; its members can still sign in to the portal, read-only, with a banner explaining the suspension.

"Open as org admin" takes you into the organisation with everything an org admin can do there, including resetting an org admin's authenticator. Every change you make there appears in that organisation's audit log, marked "(operator)".

A range an org admin adds does nothing until you approve it. From an approved range, Probation refuses every key that is not the organisation's own, so check the range really is that organisation's office or VPN. Phone networks and country-wide address sharing put unrelated people behind one address, so a range cannot identify a company on its own.
