Local-service publishing · Slice 4 complete

PyPI publishing and uv workflows

Delivered

A developer can run twine upload (Basic or Bearer credential) against the local service URL /registry/pypi/<id>/. The package-manager credential goes only to the selected registry, and the registry's status and body are returned unchanged, so the upstream stays authoritative. The firewall's own policy API key never reaches the registry.

Before, a Twine POST to that URL was refused locally (405). Now it is forwarded. Refused locally with no upstream I/O: unknown registry IDs, unrelated Python POST paths, query-bearing uploads, non-POST methods, and declared or chunked uploads over the limit (413). If the managed policy is unavailable or the service key is revoked, the upload is refused and nothing is written upstream. Uploads over 1 MiB get the 15-minute lifetime instead of the 30-second metadata deadline; a defect that returned 504 for slow large uploads was found and fixed.

Public signatures and the approved molecule flow/contracts are unchanged; no spec was weakened. Changed: the classifier in src/http/service_registry.rs, the atomic generation check plus rate charge in admit_certificate (src/managed/mod.rs, at most three asks), and UPLOAD_BODY_BYTES/selected_within in src/upstream/reqwest_transport.rs.

Limits

Recorded as open by your decision on 2026-11-04; no code change in Slice 4.

  • Upload spool and timeouts (major availability, admitted peers only). The spool cap is 2×max_artifact_bytes + max_metadata_bytes, written before the upstream credential check, with no read-idle or total client timeout and no upstream write-idle timer. A stalled client or upstream holds a permit; upstream sends are bounded at 15 minutes.
  • Post-write 503 (minor). A policy snapshot change after the upstream write commits returns 503 "no valid blocklist is loaded"; a retry then hits the upstream's "file exists".
  • One URL per registry. Uploads POST to the configured URL, so a registry whose upload endpoint differs from its Simple URL (pypi.org to upload.pypi.org/legacy/) is unsupported.
  • Advisories (header sensitivity, auth-scheme case, log throttling, registry-ID enumeration, and others) are listed in the evidence.
