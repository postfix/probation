## Behaviors to exclude from a port

[Lifecycle](https://github.com/AikidoSec/safe-chain/blob/c9ccceec379fc225e3c0fca0178e86ce4140aa2e/packages/safe-chain/src/main.js), [proxy](https://github.com/AikidoSec/safe-chain/blob/c9ccceec379fc225e3c0fca0178e86ce4140aa2e/packages/safe-chain/src/registryProxy/registryProxy.js).
