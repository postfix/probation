Delete your personal account?

Your personal account's keys stop working within about 1 minute. Everything else in it is erased after 30 days; until then you can restore it here in Account settings. The audit log is kept for its normal retention.

Your login stays, because it still belongs to Acme and Northwind. A login that belongs to no organisation is deleted together with its personal account; to restore both within the same 30 days, sign in again with any of its old sign-in methods and choose "Restore login".
